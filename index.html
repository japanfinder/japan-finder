How it works
From a photo to a place to buy in three simple steps.

 
STEP 01
Share a photo
Upload an image to preview the item. Use on-device AI to suggest a category, brand, and possible product name; review the suggestions.
STEP 02
Choose your preference
Choose new or used products, set a budget, and add a product name or clues.
STEP 03
Discover your options
Open relevant searches on Amazon Japan, Mercari, and Yahoo! Flea Market.




Our idea
Japan is full of things worth finding.
JAPAN FINDER aims to make discovering Japanese products easier for people everywhere — even when all they have is a picture.
